Privacy notice

What we collect through this site, why, and how to have it deleted. Last updated 18 October 2026.

Who we are

QAmeleon is run by Eamonn Doyle, trading as QAmeleon, in Ireland. In this notice "we" and "us" means Eamonn Doyle. We are the controller of the personal data collected through qameleon.ai.

For anything about your data, email hello@qameleon.ai.

The short version

  • If you join early access, we keep your email address and use it only to talk to you about QAmeleon early access.
  • This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, tracking or ads.
  • We never sell your email or share it for anyone else's marketing.
  • Email us at any time and we will delete it.

What we collect

When you join early access

Your email address, and the date and time you sent it. If you reply to us, we also keep what you choose to tell us, such as what you are building and how you test it today.

When you visit the site

Our hosting provider, Netlify, processes technical details that every website receives, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. This is needed to deliver the pages and keep the site secure. We do not use it to identify you.

The site sets no cookies, has no analytics or tracking scripts, and serves its fonts from our own address, so nothing about your visit is passed to font or advertising services.

Why we use it, and on what basis

  • Your email address: to reply to you, ask about your needs, invite you to try QAmeleon and tell you when it is available. Our legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by sending the form. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us, and we will delete your details.
  • Technical details from your visit: to run the site and protect it from misuse. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating a secure website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not add you to any other mailing list, and we do not use your data for automated decisions about you.

Who we share it with

  • Netlify, Inc., which hosts the site and stores early-access sign-ups for us.
  • Our email provider, when we write to you.
  • Anthropic, whose Claude app hosts the private list we use to keep track of sign-ups and our replies.

These providers act on our instructions and may not use your data for their own purposes. We share nothing with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Transfers outside the EU

Netlify and Anthropic are based in the United States, so your data may be processed there. Where data leaves the European Economic Area, it is protected by safeguards recognised under GDPR, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address while early access is running and we are in touch with you, and delete it no later than 24 months after our last contact, or sooner if you ask. If you become a QAmeleon customer, separate terms will cover the data needed for that. Netlify keeps routine server logs for a short time under its own retention policy.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the data we hold about you
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • send it to you, or to someone else, in a common format

Email hello@qameleon.ai. We will reply within one month. It is free.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission, Ireland's regulator, at dataprotection.ie. We would appreciate the chance to put it right first.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not aimed at anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Sign-ups are kept in our Netlify account and our private sign-up list, which only we can access.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your email address, we will tell you before it applies.
