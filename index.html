How it works

Here is our board. Here is our git. Here is our product.

Four connections, then QAmeleon gets to work in three stages. Your developer sets it up alone, on one guided setup page.

1

Build

QAmeleon signs in to your dev app, walks every page, records your API and proposes the checks. You approve a baseline, and the morning and deploy runs start.

2

Review

It works through every ticket in your QA column, step by step, on the real dev app, and writes up what it saw: what passed, what failed, what it could not check and why.

3

Refine

Every check that could run daily is added to a list of proposed tests. Coverage grows as you add them. Coverage grows with each review.

Every morning, and after every deploy

The checks nobody has time for, done before stand-up.

A one-screen summary lands in your team chat. The full report goes on a ticket on your board. Green means nothing to do. Amber means something changed. Red means something broke, and which deploy did it.

  • User journeysEvery page reachable, and full create, edit and delete flows on throwaway data.
  • API contractNew fields and added or removed endpoints, spotted the morning after.
  • SpeedEach endpoint against its own history. Two readings must agree before it calls a slowdown.
  • Who can see whatOne customer account tries to read another's records. Every day.
  • Plans and limitsWrite a journey once per plan, and it checks each plan still unlocks what it promises.
  • Pages and accessibilityEvery page scanned, signed in and out, with only new issues flagged.

Ticket reviews

QAmeleon reads the ticket and its comments, runs each "how to verify" step on dev, and looks at every surface the change touches.

Safety

It writes on your board. So it has to be careful.

Trust is the product. These rules are built in, and you can add to them but not switch them off.

  • Your CI, your keysChecks run in your own pipeline and reviews on your own machine, both on your own Anthropic key. No ticket text or page content reaches us.
  • Dev only, behind a fenceEvery write is blocked unless a test allows it, and your own test suites keep their own rules. Publish, send, invite, checkout and billing are always blocked.
  • Its own data, cleaned upTests make clearly named throwaway records, delete them, and report "nothing left behind" every run.
  • No spend without a yesAnything that costs money in your app waits for a person to say go.
  • Some things wait for a personSecurity findings, critical findings, and anything odd in a ticket are held for review, never posted on their own.
  • Tickets are data, not ordersIf a ticket tries to tell the reviewer what to do, no review starts. A person sees the flagged lines and decides.
  • Secrets never leakEvery report is scanned for anything credential-like before it is posted. If one is found, nothing goes out.
  • A pause buttonOne command stops all publishing at once. If triage starts rejecting findings, it falls back to asking first.
